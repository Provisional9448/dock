Say the load number and one company or name from the load. A role by itself never verifies. If those two do not match, Doc asks once more, then gives general information only.

“It’s LL-88421, this is Dan at Northwind Orchards.”

  1. Shipper Northwind Orchards · Dan Hears free time ends at 4:06, then $75 an hour.
  2. Receiver Harbor & Rye · Priya Hears the status, then is asked for a door and a time.

  4. Driver Red Kettle · Luis Check-in only. No money.

What the line does

  1. Verifies who’s calling, and which shipment

    Load number, plus a company or a name on that load.

  2. Logs driver check-ins and waits

    The check-in time, and what the receiver told the driver.

  3. Heads-up to the payer

    Shipper or consignee, whoever pays on the load, hears free time and detention. On LL-88421 the shipper pays.

  4. Messages the broker

    Status, the cost, and that the carrier invoices from the timestamps.

  5. Answers basic shipment questions

    A verified caller gets that role’s answers. Anyone else gets general information only.
