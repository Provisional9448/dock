# 04 — WhatsApp: "let me know when the unit is out of the yard"

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE. All names, codes and links are invented.

**Channel:** direct WhatsApp, client ops lead → Dock assistant

```
[Oct 12, 13:09] Sam: https://example.com/fleet/live/u1841-demo?share=Q4T8W
[Oct 12, 13:09] Sam: tell me when it leaves Northwind yard
[Oct 12, 15:40] Sam: ?
[Oct 12, 15:41] Sam: what time it got there?
```

## Expected follow-ups
- A one-time notice when the truck leaves the shipper yard (Northwind Orchards, Fresno). Being stopped at the gate doesn't count.
- When asked "what time it got there?", give the actual time and its source. A forecast isn't an answer.
- The client chases quickly ("?") when a promised milestone hasn't been reported, so even "no change yet, last ping 15:22" is better than silence.
