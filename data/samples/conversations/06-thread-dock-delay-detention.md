## Patterns shown
- The waiting time is raised before free time runs out, and the agreed rate is named.
- The cause of the delay comes from the receiver, not the carrier.
- Approval depends on proof: signed in/out times on the POD.
- The charge is a proposal until it's approved. It isn't treated as final.
